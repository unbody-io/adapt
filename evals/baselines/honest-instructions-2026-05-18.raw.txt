    Q: How do I make pour-over coffee?
       relevant=true relevance=1 confidence=0.9
       insight: To make pour-over coffee, follow these steps:

1. **Gather Equipment and Ingredients**:
   - Pour-over brewer (like a V60 or Chemex)
   - Coffee filter (make sure it fits your brewer)
   - Fresh coffee beans (medium-fine grind, about 2 tablespoons per 6 ounces of water)
   - Hot water kettle (preferably with a gooseneck for precision)
   - Scale (optional but helpful for accuracy)
   - Coffee grinder (if using whole beans)

2. **Heat Water**:
   - Boil water, then let it cool slightly (ideal temperature is between 195°F and 205°F).

3. **Prepare the Brewer**:
   - Place the filter in your pour-over brewer and rinse it with hot water. This removes paper flavors and preheats the brewer.

4. **Add Coffee Grounds**:
   - Measure and add the medium-fine coffee grounds to the filter. A common ratio is about 1:16 (1 gram of coffee to 16 grams of water).

5. **Bloom the Coffee**:
   - Pour a small amount of hot water (about twice the weight of the coffee) over the grounds to saturate them evenly. Let it bloom for about 30-45 seconds. This helps release trapped gases and enhances flavor extraction.

6. **Continue Pouring**:
   - After blooming, slowly pour the remaining water over the coffee in a circular motion. Avoid pouring directly onto the filter. Aim to complete this process in about 2-3 minutes.

7. **Let it Drip**:
   - Allow the coffee to fully drain through the filter.

8. **Serve and Enjoy**:
   - Remove the filter and discard the grounds. Pour the brewed coffee into your favorite mug and enjoy. You can adjust your grind size and water temperature for taste preferences.

This method produces a clean and nuanced coffee that showcases the beans' flavors. Experiment with different coffee origins and roast profiles to discover a variety of tasting notes.
