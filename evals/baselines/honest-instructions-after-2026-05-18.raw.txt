   - Pour-over brewer (like a Hario V60, Chemex, or similar)

   - Fresh coffee beans (medium-fine ground)

2. **Measure Coffee and Water**:

   - Place the filter in the brewer and rinse it with hot water. This pre-wets the filter and removes any paper taste.

4. **Add Coffee Grounds**:

6. **Pour Water**:

7. **Let It Drip**:
   - Allow the coffee to completely drip through the filter into your vessel below. This might take another minute or so.

8. **Enjoy Your Coffee**:
